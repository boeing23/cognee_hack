# The Challenge

> Build a brain. Build an agent. Then make it do something.

In today's world, an AI agent can know what you know — your emails, documents,
meetings, calendar, tasks, files — and then act on it.

## 1. Build your Personal Brain

Your agent is only as useful as the information it can understand. Create an
AI-powered brain that combines knowledge from multiple sources:

- Your laptop and local files
- Google Drive and cloud documents
- Personal email and other data sources
- Public information from across the web
- Meeting notes, receipts, photos, schedules, and more

Use **Cognee's** structured AI memory for the brain and **Bright Data** to
discover and retrieve public web data.

## 2. Build your Agent

Once the agent can access the right knowledge, build one that can **find
information, reason across it, and take action**.

### Example agents

1. **Manage your calendar** — find availability and schedule appointments automatically
2. **Take notes and remind you later** — capture info and send a reminder exactly when needed
3. **Turn meetings into action** — read summaries, identify commitments/action items, build a to-do schedule
4. **Handle your expenses** — process receipts, extract key info, organize, prepare expense reports
5. **Organize and share your photos** — process photos, upload to Google Photos, organize, share to a WhatsApp group
6. **Build your own personal assistant** — understands your info, remembers what matters, helps you get things done
7. **Connect your digital life** — connect apps/services, read info, create notes, identify what matters, remind you when action is required

Or build something completely different. **Your brain. Your agent. Your idea.**

## The Battle

This isn't about building another chatbot. The goal is agents that can
**understand information and then accomplish something useful**.

At the end, each team demos what their agent can do. Judging themes:

- Will it remember better?
- Find better information?
- Reason across personal, company, and public data?
- Take useful actions?

> Can your brain beat the other brains?

## The core equation

**Data + Memory + Reasoning + Tools + Actions**
